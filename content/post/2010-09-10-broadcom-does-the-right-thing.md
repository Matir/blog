<p>Looks like Broadcom is doing the right thing: <a href="http://arstechnica.com/open-source/news/2010/09/broadcom-announces-official-open-source-drivers-for-linux.ars">http://arstechnica.com/open-source/news/2010/09/broadcom-announces-official-open-source-drivers-for-linux.ars</a></p>
<p>They've released fully-open drivers for 3 of their 802.11n chipsets.  I hope this'll spread to more of their hardware, but regardless, it's a great move.  No longer will Broadcom be an absolute contraindication to my buying hardware.  Thanks Broadcom!</p>
